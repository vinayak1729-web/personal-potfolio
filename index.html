    I'm a quantitative developer and researcher who owns the full loop — from the exchange's raw feed, to the signal computed on it, to the decision of when that signal should not be traded.

    I started with rule-based option structures and moved steadily toward ideas borrowed from physics and statistics: treating the order book as a system with mass and momentum, measuring whether price is moving with direction or just diffusing, and letting thresholds adapt to the market instead of fixing them by hand.

    I ship that research as production software in C++ and Python — deterministic, monitored and fast enough to matter. Outside the desk I publish QuantResearch on PyPI and run The Algorithmic Forge on YouTube.
